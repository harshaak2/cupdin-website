08Who qualifies as a cafe

Cupdin is meant for real, publicly operating cafes, coffee shops, tea houses, and similar food-and-beverage spots people can walk into and order from. To keep the map useful and trustworthy, a place listed on Cupdin should be:

  • A real, operating business. It has a physical location, is currently open to the public, and serves food or drinks on-site.
  • Independently verifiable. Its name and location can be reasonably confirmed to exist — we may remove listings we can't verify.
  • Not a private residence or closed venue. Home setups, private events, or members-only spaces that aren't generally open to the public don't qualify.
  • Not a duplicate. If a cafe already exists in the app, please log visits against the existing listing rather than creating a new one.
